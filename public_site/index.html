Built to grow responsibly

Our direction is to provide transaction-risk infrastructure without holding customer funds. Banks and authorized payment providers remain responsible for payment execution.

TrustNG's capabilities are under active development and verification. No claim is made that every fraud attempt can be detected or prevented. Production use requires appropriate testing, security review, and integration approvals.
