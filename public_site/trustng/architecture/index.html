TrustNG Architecture

Prototype foundation

Input and API layer

Receives structured requests through the application's API and validates the information supplied for assessment.

Prototype capability

Risk assessment layer

Evaluates transaction and recipient information against the risk logic currently implemented in the project.

Prototype capability

Policy and advisory layer

Organises assessment results into advisory outcomes and supporting information. The consuming institution retains decision authority.

Prototype capability

Integrity and records layer

Uses database-backed records, idempotency, audit and reconciliation mechanisms to support traceability and safer handling of uncertain operations.

Planned

External provider connections

Bank, payment, identity and other third-party connections are future integration work. No unapproved or unimplemented provider connection should be assumed.

TrustNG is an early-stage risk-assessment and decision-support project. It does not hold customer funds, execute payments or independently authorise or block bank transactions. Prototype and sandbox capabilities are not production validation, licensing or approved bank integrations. All risk and policy outcomes are advisory.
