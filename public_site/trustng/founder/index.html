Founder

Founder, TrustNG

Orim Busa-Osowo Paul

TrustNG was founded to explore how better risk intelligence and trust infrastructure can support the future of digital financial services across Africa.

Purpose

The vision

The project focuses on helping financial service providers assess transaction signals, understand potential risks and make better-informed security decisions.

Approach

Responsible development

The work is grounded in practical technology, careful system design, transparent development stages and collaboration with the financial services ecosystem.

TrustNG is an early-stage risk-assessment and decision-support project. It does not hold customer funds, execute payments or independently authorise or block bank transactions. Prototype and sandbox capabilities are not production validation, licensing or approved bank integrations. All risk and policy outcomes are advisory.
