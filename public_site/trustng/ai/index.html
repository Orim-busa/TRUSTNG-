Platform information

AI Risk Intelligence

Prototype capability

Advisory analysis

TrustNG's AI risk-intelligence component is designed to provide supplementary analysis of transaction risk information.

Core principle

Human and institutional control

AI output remains advisory. It does not independently authorise transactions, move funds or replace the controls of a financial institution.

Development priority

Explainability and evidence

Risk assessments should be supported by understandable reasons and traceable inputs wherever possible. AI confidence should not be represented as calibrated unless it has been properly validated.

Ongoing requirement

Testing and validation

Before any production use, models and rules require appropriate testing, evaluation, monitoring, security assessment and review for their intended environment.

TrustNG is an early-stage risk-assessment and decision-support project. It does not hold customer funds, execute payments or independently authorise or block bank transactions. Prototype and sandbox capabilities are not production validation, licensing or approved bank integrations. All risk and policy outcomes are advisory.
