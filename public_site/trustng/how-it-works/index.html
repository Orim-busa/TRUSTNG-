How TrustNG Works

Input stage

01. Information received

A participating system supplies transaction information and permitted recipient signals for assessment. The information available depends on the integration and the permissions governing its use.

Assessment stage

02. Risk evaluated

TrustNG evaluates relevant indicators using its risk-assessment logic and available trust signals. The quality of the result depends on the completeness and reliability of the supplied information.

Advisory stage

03. Policy recommendation

The system returns a risk assessment and an advisory policy outcome, with supporting reasons where available. The recommendation is not an instruction to move money.

Decision stage

04. Responsible review

The financial institution or authorised service provider retains responsibility for its own decisions, customer controls and payment systems. TrustNG does not execute the transaction.

TrustNG is an early-stage risk-assessment and decision-support project. It does not hold customer funds, execute payments or independently authorise or block bank transactions. Prototype and sandbox capabilities are not production validation, licensing or approved bank integrations. All risk and policy outcomes are advisory.
