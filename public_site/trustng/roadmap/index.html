How it could work: It could compare identity attributes with permitted PEP datasets, distinguish possible matches from confirmed matches and support enhanced review without automatically determining a customer's legal status.

Who benefits: Financial institutions, payment providers, developers, risk teams or customers, depending on the use case.

Suspicious Activity Reporting Support

Planned

What it is: A proposed workflow capability to help authorised compliance teams organise evidence and prepare information relevant to suspicious activity reporting obligations.

How it could work: It could assemble linked transaction records, risk indicators, investigation notes and timelines into reviewable case material, with access controls and audit records; any decision to submit a report would remain with the responsible institution and its authorised personnel.

Important product boundary

TrustNG is being developed as a financial risk-intelligence and decision-support product. It does not hold customer funds, move money, execute payments or independently authorize or block bank transactions. Payment-related roadmap ideas would depend on authorized financial institutions and payment providers.

“Current prototype” means related functionality exists in the present project in a limited form. It does not mean independently audited, production-ready, connected to live financial networks or commercially available. Planned, research and concept items are not currently available services.
