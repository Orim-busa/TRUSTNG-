01 / The mission

Building a stronger layer of trust for digital finance.

Digital financial services need more than transaction speed. They need ways to assess risk, understand recipient signals and support responsible decisions before transactions proceed.

01 — THE CHALLENGE

Fragmented trust signals

Relevant identity, transaction and behavioural signals can be difficult to evaluate together.

02 — THE APPROACH

Risk intelligence

TrustNG is developing a structured layer for risk assessment and policy recommendations.

03 — THE DIRECTION

Connected financial systems

Our long-term direction is to support responsible integration with financial service providers.

02 / Technology

A modular approach to transaction trust.

The platform is being developed as a collection of connected risk and trust capabilities.

Prototype capability

Transaction risk assessment

Evaluates supplied transaction information and identifies signals that may require additional review.

Prototype capability

Policy recommendations

Produces advisory outcomes such as ALLOW, REVIEW or RESTRICT for consideration by an authorised decision-maker.

Prototype capability

AI risk intelligence

Provides an additional advisory analysis of transaction risk signals. It does not independently authorise transactions.

Prototype capability

Recipient trust workflows

Sandbox-oriented recipient and account-trust workflows designed to explore identity-related risk signals.

Prototype capability

Transaction integrity

Idempotency, audit and reconciliation mechanisms designed to support safer system behaviour.

Development direction

Integration infrastructure

Future work includes partner integrations, expanded verification signals and broader developer tooling.

03 / The pipeline

From transaction signals to informed decisions.

TrustNG is designed to provide a risk-intelligence layer alongside the systems that financial institutions already operate.

01
Information received

Transaction and permitted recipient signals are supplied for assessment.

02
Risk evaluated

Risk indicators and relevant trust signals are assessed.

03
Policy recommendation

The system returns an advisory outcome and supporting reasons.

04
Responsible review

The authorised institution or service provider retains control of its decisions and payment systems.

06 / Responsible development

Trust intelligence—not payment execution.

TrustNG is being developed as a risk-assessment and decision-support platform. It does not hold customer funds, execute payments or independently authorise or block bank transactions.

TrustNG is an early-stage project with prototype and sandbox-oriented capabilities. It is not presented as a production-validated fraud prevention service, a licensed financial institution or an approved bank integration. All risk and policy outcomes are advisory. Production deployment requires appropriate validation, security assessment, partner integration and regulatory review.
