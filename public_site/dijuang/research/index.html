Research and Publications

Research helps us understand problems before deciding how technology should address them.

Explore our work Contact DIJUANG

Risk and trust systems

Areas of interest include how organisations assess risk, interpret available signals and make accountable decisions in complex digital environments.

Identity and verification

Research may examine the distinction between control of an email address or phone number, legal identity, recipient confidence and authority to carry out a transaction.

Artificial intelligence

We are interested in appropriate uses of AI for analysis and decision support, including explainability, evaluation, limitations and human oversight.

Security and resilience

Relevant topics include secure system design, access control, audit trails, idempotency, reconciliation and the handling of uncertain system outcomes.
