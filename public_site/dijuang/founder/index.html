Orim Busa-Osowo Paul

Founder of DIJUANG. The company is being built around a long-term interest in purposeful technology, protection and practical solutions to real-world problems.

The founding direction

DIJUANG is being developed as a technology company with the ambition to create useful products for connected communities and markets. The founding approach places importance on careful execution, responsible development and building credibility through working systems.

Building the first product

TrustNG is the first product being developed under DIJUANG. Its focus is transaction risk intelligence: helping organisations assess information and identify risk indicators before making their own decisions.

A work in progress

DIJUANG is at an early stage. The company will communicate its progress and capabilities accurately as products are developed, tested and validated.
