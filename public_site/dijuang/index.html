The meaning behind the name

More than a name.
A foundation.

DIJUANG comes from the Osokom language. Its meaning, as understood in its cultural context, is connected to a protected natural spring where people collect clean drinking water — a place valued and safeguarded by the community.

That idea gives the company its foundation: protect what is valuable, build with purpose, and create technology people can place their trust in.

Our first product · By DIJUANG

TrustNG

Transaction risk intelligence designed to help businesses assess transaction and recipient risk before making decisions. TrustNG is being developed with a focus on risk signals, identity confidence and clearer risk assessment.

EXPLORE TRUSTNG

TrustNG is an early-stage product. Its current capabilities are advisory and risk-assessment focused; it does not authorize or execute payments or move funds.
