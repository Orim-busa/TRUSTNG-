Technology and Innovation

Technology should be useful, understandable and developed with responsibility.

Explore our work Contact DIJUANG

Applied problem-solving

We focus on applying technology to clearly defined problems. The objective is not to use technology for its own sake, but to develop systems that can provide practical value.

Security and system integrity

We consider access control, data protection, reliable system behaviour, auditability and the ability to investigate uncertain outcomes as important engineering concerns.

Artificial intelligence

AI may support analysis and decision assistance where it is appropriate. AI outputs must be evaluated, their limitations understood and meaningful human or institutional control preserved.

Research and validation

New approaches require testing and evidence. A concept, prototype or demonstration is not the same as a validated production system.

Responsible innovation

Our development direction recognises privacy, safety, regulatory obligations and the consequences that technology can have for people and organisations.
