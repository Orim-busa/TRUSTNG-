About DIJUANG

A technology company built around protection, trust and purposeful innovation.

Explore our work Contact DIJUANG

Who we are

DIJUANG is a technology company focused on developing useful solutions to meaningful problems. Our approach is grounded in protection, responsible design and the belief that technology should earn the confidence people place in it.

Why we exist

We aim to identify important problems and develop thoughtful technology that helps people, organisations and connected markets address them. We value practical usefulness over promises that cannot yet be demonstrated.

How we build

We approach development with research, testing, transparency and a clear distinction between what exists today and what remains planned. We intend to grow through solutions that demonstrate real value.

Our first product

TrustNG is DIJUANG's first product. It is an early-stage transaction risk-intelligence project focused on advisory risk assessment and decision support. It does not move funds or execute payments.
