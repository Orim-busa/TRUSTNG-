The Meaning of DIJUANG

A name rooted in the idea of a protected source: something valuable that a community cares for.

Explore our work Contact DIJUANG

The Osokom connection

DIJUANG comes from the Osokom language. In its cultural context, the name is connected to a protected natural spring where community members collect clean drinking water. It is a place valued and safeguarded by the community.

A place with a purpose

The spring is understood as a source of drinking water, not a place for bathing. Its protection reflects the importance of preserving something that serves a shared need.

The idea behind the company

That meaning gives DIJUANG a guiding idea: protect what is valuable, respect what people depend on, and build with purpose. It is a foundation for how we think about technology and responsibility.

From meaning to practice

The name is not a claim that technology can guarantee safety or trust. It is a reminder of the standard we want our work to pursue: useful solutions, careful development and respect for the people and systems they affect.
